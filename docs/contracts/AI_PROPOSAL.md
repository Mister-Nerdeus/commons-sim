# AI Proposal

AiDesignProposal records editable proposal-plane artifacts. Proposals carry prompt, seed, mode, lifecycle status, warnings, provenance, confidence, and validation status.

AI content may be accepted, rejected, remixed, or superseded, but it does not become simulation truth by being generated. ModePolicy remains the validation boundary.

Non-claims:

- No external AI calls are implemented.
- No provider interface is implemented.
- No AI proposal bypasses validation.
- No draft proposal is professional approval.
