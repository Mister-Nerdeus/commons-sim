1) Phase 1 prioritizes transparency over calibration.
2) Utility and labor coefficients are placeholders; they will be replaced by:
   - equipment capacity models
   - throughput limits
   - shift coverage
   - empirical utility baselines
3) No capex financing model yet (cash-only annualization).
4) No queueing theory yet (peak demand vs average demand).
5) Burnout is a proxy; a real staffing coverage model comes in Phase 2.
